About This Album

Recorded over three consecutive evenings in Bombay, Santoor Recital Vol. III is widely considered the definitive document of Pandit Shivkumar Sharma's mature style. The santoor — a hundred-stringed hammered dulcimer — sings here with an intimacy that belies the instrument's percussive origins. Each raga unfolds across its allotted time with patient inevitability, never hurrying toward resolution.

Pandit Shivkumar Sharma (13 January 1938 – 10 May 2022) was an Indian classical musician who played the santoor and is credited with establishing it as a serious Hindustani classical music instrument. Born in Jammu, he was introduced to music by his father Uma Dutt Sharma, who was a vocalist and tabla player.

Over six decades of performance, Sharma elevated the santoor — once considered a folk instrument of the Kashmir Valley — into the concert halls of the world. His collaboration with flautist Hariprasad Chaurasia under the name Shiv-Hari also produced some of the most beloved Bollywood soundtracks of the 1980s.
